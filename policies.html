Store policies

Last updated September 25, 2026

Shipping

We ship to every province and territory in Canada and to the United States. Shipping is free on all orders.

  • Processing: 1 to 3 business days after you order.
  • Delivery: most orders arrive 7 to 15 business days after they ship. Remote areas can take longer.
  • Tracking: you get a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships.
  • Items in one order may arrive in separate packages.

If your order hasn't arrived 25 business days after it shipped, email us and we will send a replacement or a full refund.

Returns & refunds

Damaged, faulty or wrong item: email us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We will send a replacement or refund you in full, your choice. You don't need to send the item back.

Changed your mind: unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Email us first and we will send the return address. Return shipping is paid by the customer. Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 to 10 business days after we receive the item.

For hygiene reasons, opened beauty and personal care products can only be returned if they are damaged or faulty.

Privacy

We collect only what we need to fill your order: your name, email, shipping address and what you bought.

  • Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details go straight to Stripe and we never see or store them.
  • Your shipping details are shared only with the suppliers and couriers who deliver your order.
  • Analytics: we use Google Analytics to see how visitors use the site. It uses cookies and does not tell us who you are.
  • We never sell your information.

To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, email qafizz@qafizz.com. We handle personal information in line with Canada's PIPEDA.

Terms

By ordering from Qafizz you agree to these terms.

  • Prices are in Canadian dollars. Any sales tax that applies is shown at checkout.
  • Product photos are for illustration. Colours can look slightly different on your screen.
  • We may cancel and fully refund an order if an item is out of stock or was listed at the wrong price.
  • Use products as the instructions say. Patch test beauty products before first use.
  • These terms are governed by the laws of the province of Ontario and of Canada.

Contact

Email qafizz@qafizz.com. We reply within 1 to 2 business days.
