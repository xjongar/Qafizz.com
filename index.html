Questions

How long does shipping take?

Orders are processed in 1 to 3 business days, then most arrive in 7 to 15 business days. You get a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships.

Where do you ship?

Everywhere in Canada and the United States.

Is checkout safe?

Yes. Payments are handled by Stripe, the same processor used by millions of online stores. Your card details go straight to Stripe and never touch our site.

What if my item arrives damaged or wrong?

Email us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We will send a replacement or refund you, your choice.

Can I return something I changed my mind about?

Unused items in original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Email us first and we will send return instructions. Return shipping is paid by the customer unless the item was faulty.

Do prices include tax?

Prices are in Canadian dollars. Any sales tax that applies is shown at checkout before you pay.
